# WP17: the portal's new look

Branch `claude/portal-redesign`. The change is visual only. No page's data, calls or permissions changed. The owner gave the direction: octave's soft cards and layout, with Nothing's type discipline and moving dot-matrix motif.

Screenshots (1440x960 at 2x, from `npm run dev:mock`) are in `wp17/`:
- `migrations-light.png` and `migrations-dark.png`: the console;
- `annex-light.png` and `annex-dark.png`: an Annex page;
- `signin-light.png` and `signin-dark.png`: the screen shown while the session loads.

## Tokens (`portal/src/theme/`)

- `fonts.css`: Space Grotesk 400 and 500 (UI), Space Mono 400 (labels and data), Doto 700 (hero numbers only).
  - Latin, plus Latin Extended loaded only when a name needs it (`unicode-range`).
  - woff2 only: 88 kB across 7 files, bundled into `dist/assets`.
  - They come from the pinned `@fontsource/*` 5.3.0 packages (OFL-1.1, rows in `LICENSES.md`). Vite rewrites each `url()` to a hashed file of the portal's own origin.
- `tokens.css`: every colour is a plain hex value.
  - Light: a warm light-grey page (`#f3f2ef`), white cards, hairline borders (`#e6e4df`) and black ink.
  - Dark: OLED black, cards `#0f0f0f`, ink `#ededed`.
  - Primary actions are ink, not blue. Links are `--link`.
  - Colour is kept for data and status: the tier ramp, ok, warn and danger, and one signal red (`#d71921`). The red marks what needs a person, and the one accent series in a chart.
  - Older names (`--ink`, `--muted`, `--accent`…) are kept and point at the new palette, so feature CSS follows without edits.
- Type scale: label 11 px (mono caps, 0.08 em), caption 12, small 13, body 14, lead 16, heading 18, title 34, hero 72 (Doto).
- Spacing: a 4 px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64).
- Radius: 6 (technical), 8, 14 (cards), and pill for buttons, filter pills and tags.
- `motion.css`:
  - Durations: 90, 140, 220 and 380 ms.
  - Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)` (ease-out) and `cubic-bezier(0.16, 0.84, 0.3, 1)` (entrances). Neither overshoots; a test checks every curve stays within 0..1.
  - A page fades and rises 8 px. Its first five blocks follow 45 ms apart, with `backwards` fill so nothing stays transformed afterwards.
  - Only transform and opacity animate. The current recall step's ring scales and fades; before, it animated `box-shadow`.
  - `prefers-reduced-motion`: no entrances, no loops.

## The dot layer (`theme/dots.ts`, `theme/DotField.tsx`)

A dependency-free canvas. Dots are 1.6 px on a 7 px grid. The field drifts as three slow interfering waves. A ripple starts on each navigation, and dots within 84 px of the pointer brighten and lean away.

It can also draw a glyph in dots. The text is rasterized offscreen in Space Grotesk and sampled at each grid point. Once the fonts have loaded, the glyph is drawn again from the real face.

It is used in three places:
- the shell: one live layer behind every page head, masked to fade left and down;
- empty states: a glyph ("0", "404") assembles, then rests;
- the sign-in screen: "OMNIFILE" in dots.

Hero numbers are real text in Doto, so they stay readable and accessible.

Budget:
- at most about 30 frames a second, one pass over the dots, and at most 16 fills a frame;
- it stops when the tab is hidden (`visibilitychange`) or the canvas is off screen (`IntersectionObserver`);
- under reduced motion it draws one still frame and ignores the pointer;
- it is `aria-hidden` and takes no pointer events. Colours are re-read when the theme changes.

## The shell and components

- **Sidebar:** sections under mono caps labels.
  - Overview: Files, Activity.
  - Data: Shares, Policies, Annexes, Migrations.
  - Operations: Recalls, Proposals, Audit, Edges.
  - Admin: Settings.
  - Each item keeps its old permission check, and an empty section is not drawn.
  - Annexes has its own icon now; before, it reused the Migrations icon.
- **Top bar:** the section crumb, plus a search box with ⌘K (Ctrl K elsewhere).
  - The portal has no search, so the box jumps to a page: the sidebar's pages and the sub-pages they already link to, filtered by the same permissions.
  - It is an ARIA combobox: arrows move, Enter opens, Escape closes.
- **`PageHead`:** a 34 px title, a one-line question as its subtitle, the old lede kept under it, actions top right, and one Doto hero number per page where the page already has the data:
  - Migrations: % of bytes verified;
  - Annex: % verified in Azure;
  - Edges: reporting OK;
  - Recalls: under way;
  - Proposals: waiting;
  - Activity: bytes committed.
- **Tabs:** Migrations (Console, Agents, Plans) and Settings show their sub-pages as browser-like tabs. They are the same links as before.
- **Filter pills:** native selects and inputs dressed as pills, used where pages already filter. Labels and control types are unchanged, so tests and assistive technology see the same controls:
  - Annex: Window, Path, Show;
  - Activity: Dataset, Window;
  - Proposals: Show, Dataset.
- **Findings cards:**
  - Migrations' "Needs attention" and the Annex "Problems" are ranked lists with a tag (Act now, Act soon, Watch, Check, Note) and a link with an arrow.
  - The order is by urgency, and the server's order is kept within each level.
  - Text and accessible names are unchanged.
- **Charts:** the Annex timeline draws arrivals in grey and departures in the signal red, with copies as ink ticks. It adds faint gridlines, a dashed daily-average line with its value in a pill, the first and last dates, and a legend. `<progress>` is a segmented bar.
- **Accessibility, improved here:**
  - Form control borders now meet 3:1 (`--control-line`); before, they were about 1.9:1.
  - Tier segments in dark mode use black text; before, white on light tier colours was under 3:1.
  - Focus rings are 2 px ink (white in dark).

## Polish after the first look (coordinator, 2026-11-04)

- **One line a cell in dense tables.** A new `.table-dense` style has tighter cells, and its headers may take two lines so the data keeps one.
  - On Migrations, "Prefixes being moved" now shows the prefix, the phase, a bar with "9,100 / 15,000 · 437 GB", the rate, a short ETA ("Nov 5, 12:22 AM") and "2021 · Nov 6".
  - The detail moved to each cell's tooltip and to the row's expanded panel: the dataset, the copier, files an hour, the ETA's basis and when it was counted. Screen readers still read it in the cell.
  - Sources, Edges, "Jobs under way" and a plan's Waves get the same treatment.
  - Long text (a phase, a source's last problem, an edge's reason) ends in an ellipsis, with the full text in the tooltip.
  - At 1440 px no table on these pages scrolls sideways (checked by script: `scrollWidth` equals the wrapper's width).
- **No intro that repeats the question.** Where a lede only restated the question, it is now one line saying something the question doesn't, on 17 pages. Detail pages keep their facts, such as a plan's state or a share's protocols.
