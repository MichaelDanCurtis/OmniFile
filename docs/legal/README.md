The text below is as written for decision 25.

OmniFile Gateway is licensed under the Business Source License 1.1, with `fsal/` under the LGPL-3.0 and
`samba-vfs/` under the GPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`, `LICENSES.md`, decision 25). AiLuxis Research sells
commercial licences, so it must hold enough rights in every line of the code to do that. Decision 25
therefore requires a contributor licence agreement before any outside contribution is accepted. Until
counsel has reviewed these drafts and the owner has adopted one, **no outside contribution is
accepted** (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).

These are working drafts for that review, written by the engineering team in plain language. None of
them is in effect, and none has been checked by a lawyer.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `individual-cla.md` | A contributor licence agreement for a person contributing on their own behalf. |
| `entity-cla.md` | The same for a company or other organisation whose employees contribute. |
| `dco.md` | The alternative: the Developer Certificate of Origin, and what it would and wouldn't give. |
| `questions-for-counsel.md` | What we need counsel to answer before the first public release. |

The drafts are written fresh, in our own words, to the usual pattern of such agreements (a copyright
licence, a patent licence, and the contributor's promises). Counsel may prefer to start from a
published standard form instead; that is the first question in `questions-for-counsel.md`.

`docs/legal/check.sh` checks that every draft here carries the heading above.
