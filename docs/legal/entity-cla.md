# OmniFile Gateway Entity Contributor Licence Agreement (draft 0.1)

For a company or other organisation (the "Entity") whose employees or contractors contribute to OmniFile
Gateway as part of their work. The Entity gives AiLuxis Research the rights below in what those people
contribute, so that AiLuxis Research can distribute it under the project's licences and under
commercial licences. The Entity keeps the copyright; it gives a licence, not an assignment. Each person
contributing for the Entity is listed in Schedule A. Someone contributing on their own account signs the
Individual Contributor Licence Agreement instead.

## 1. Definitions

- **"We", "us"**: AiLuxis Research, the licensor of OmniFile Gateway, and its successors and assigns.
- **"You", "the Entity"**: the organisation that signs this agreement, together with every organisation
  that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under common control with it. "Control" means owning more
  than half of the voting shares or interests, or having the power to direct its management.
- **"Designated Contributors"**: the people listed in Schedule A, as the Entity updates it.
- **"Project"**, **"Contribution"** and **"Submit"** mean what they mean in the Individual Contributor
  Licence Agreement, with "you" read as a Designated Contributor acting for the Entity.

## 2. Copyright licence

The Entity grants us, and everyone who receives software or documentation we distribute, a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of,
publicly display, publicly perform, distribute and sublicense the Contributions its Designated
Contributors submit, and derivative works of them.

That licence includes the right to license those Contributions, alone or as part of the Project, under
any terms we choose: the Business Source License 1.1 with any parameters we set for a version, the open
source licence a version converts to on its Change Date, commercial licences we sell, and, for `fsal/`
and `samba-vfs/`, those directories' own licences as well.

## 3. Patent licence

The Entity grants us, and everyone who receives software we distribute, a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated in this section) patent licence to make, have
made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer the Project, where the licence applies only
to the patent claims the Entity can license that are necessarily infringed by a Contribution alone, or by
a Contribution combined with the Project as it was when it was submitted.

If anyone starts patent litigation alleging that a Contribution, or the Project with it, infringes a
patent, the patent licences that person received under this agreement for that Contribution end on the
date the litigation is filed.

## 4. What the Entity promises

1. **Authority.** The person signing for the Entity is authorised to bind it to this agreement.
2. **Its rights.** The Entity owns, or has the right to license as this agreement says, the rights in
   each Contribution its Designated Contributors submit, and each Designated Contributor is authorised to
   submit Contributions for it.
3. **Third-party material is marked.** A Contribution that includes material the Entity didn't create
   says so when it is submitted, with its source, its licence and any restriction the Entity knows of.
4. **No known conflict.** To the Entity's knowledge, no Contribution infringes anyone's patent,
   copyright, trademark or trade secret.
5. **The list stays current.** The Entity's point of contact updates Schedule A when someone should
   start or stop contributing for it. Removing someone doesn't withdraw the licences for what they had
   already submitted.
6. **It will tell us of changes** that make any of these promises inaccurate.

## 5. No obligation, no warranty

We don't have to use any Contribution. The Entity doesn't have to support its Contributions. Unless the
law requires otherwise, or the Entity agrees in writing, Contributions are provided "as is", without
warranties or conditions of any kind, express or implied.

## 6. Everything else

- This agreement covers every Contribution the Designated Contributors have submitted for the Entity,
  and every one they submit after, until the Entity tells us in writing that it no longer covers new
  Contributions. The licences already given stay in force.
- We may assign our rights under this agreement to a successor to the Project.
- This agreement is governed by the laws of **[jurisdiction: for counsel]**.
- If part of this agreement can't be enforced, the rest still applies.
